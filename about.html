Meet Riverbend Yoga Studio

We support a steady, welcoming practice where students can ask questions, take breaks, and explore movement at their own pace.

Our instructors

Maya Chen

Maya leads gentle and restorative sessions with clear explanations and time to settle into each movement. She welcomes questions from first-time students and those returning to practice.

Jordan Ellis

Jordan focuses on vinyasa and private instruction, helping students connect breathing with movement and explore manageable sequences around their individual goals.

Accessibility accommodations

Ask about chair-based variations, props, slower transitions, and additional verbal explanations before choosing a class.

Tell us about step-free access, seating, or other accommodation needs so we can confirm what is available before your visit.

Studio policies

  • Arrive early enough to settle in before practice begins.
  • Silence phones and respect other students' space and privacy.
  • Ask permission before photographing anyone in the studio.
  • Let your instructor know if you prefer no hands-on adjustments.
  • Contact the studio if you need to cancel or change a booking. Confirm cancellation terms before reserving a paid session.
